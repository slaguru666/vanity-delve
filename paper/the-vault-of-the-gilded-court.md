# THE VAULT OF THE GILDED COURT

> *They were buried smiling. They are still smiling.*

**VANITY · Barrow · Depth 2 · 4 heroes · six areas · ~3 hours**
**v2 — 2026-08-04. Absorbs playtest-01 fixes 1–8. Playtests: 1 of 2.**

DELVE build-order **step 1**. §11 says what it is measuring.

**v2 changelog:** Queen's Grit 10→6, added Skeletons cut, silvered blade added to A5 · flattery
gate 2 heroes→1, now repeatable per area with a visible signal · Banes now bank from flattery,
mirrors and relic use, with a running tab header · both Observation clues automatic on entry ·
Ghoul defence 3→2 · A4 cut to one Ghoul · A3 gang breaks after one round · new §2b PC numbers.
*Area count deliberately held at 6 so pass 2 measures the fixes, not a shorter delve.*

---

## 1. Design intent

1. **The monsters can be handled by feeding their vanity** — and it costs you, in Banes.
2. **Procedural approach, authored climax.** Areas 1–5 are DELVE's shape; Area 6 is the seam.
3. **One motif, escalating: being looked at.**
4. **The tab should read six during the finale.** v1 failed this outright — Banes are now banked
   by *actions*, not by hoping for Stumbles.
5. **Deliberately not** a fight-through. Two planned fights in six areas, both avoidable.

**Content warnings:** live burial, self-mutilation (implied, off-screen), body horror.

---

## 2. Master rules block (stand-in GM crash course)

- **Rolls:** pool of d6. **5–6 = Success.** Most tasks need 1; hard ones 2+.
- **Damage:** a hit is **1 Grit, +1 per extra Success spent**. **Spells deal 0 base** — a mage's
  damage comes only from extras, so spells are for effects, not attrition.
- **Stumble:** failing with 2+ 1s banks a **Bane**. At **six → the Reckoning** (2d6, §21b), tab
  wipes. *Do not rely on this for pressure — see the tab header.*
- **Push:** reroll non-Successes, **+1 Bane**.
- **Vanity:** spend 1 for **+1 die**, cap 6. Gaining it is a reward; the bill is a Bane.
- **The Draw:** initiative is 1d6, rerolled every round.

## 3. The skeleton (GM truth)

**The prize.** *Dorian's ever-young portrait* — the Queen's likeness, painted while she lived.

**The bottom problem.** **The Gilded Queen**, a Wraith who cannot see herself. She interred her
court alive so someone would always tell her what she looks like.

**The factions.** The **Silent Host** (Skeletons, Ghouls) — court ritual on a loop; *courtesy,
not violence*. The **Quill Gang** — four living grave-robbers, three days in, out of oil and nerve,
led by **Pell Quill**.

**The motif.** Being looked at. **The recurring danger.** Attention.

**Timeline.** 300 years ago the Queen dies and takes her court with her. 40 years ago the barrow
is sealed. **Three days ago** the Quill Gang breaks in and wakes the Host. **Tonight** the party
arrives — the barrow is already stirred, which is why the clock starts warm.

## 5. The clock — Wandering the Dark

Every **3 Turns**, or on attention, roll **1d6**. **On a 1 — *something comes***; roll Reaction
for mood. *v1 note: this fired zero times in four rolls. If it has not fired by Area 4, roll it on
a **1–2** thereafter.*

**Telegraph one Turn ahead:**
> *(read aloud or paraphrase)* Somewhere below, cloth moves on stone. Something has adjusted its
> posture, the way a person does when they are about to be looked at.

**Attention triggers:** the Court Door, being reflected twice in A3, a raised voice, striking a
Skeleton that had not moved.

## 6. The areas

**Cue** (players) · **Truth** (GM) · then decision, temptation, failure case. Mechanics inline.

---

### AREA 1 — The Mourners' Steps
*Approach · no encounter · Banes: —*

> *(read aloud or paraphrase)* Forty steps down, and every one has a mourner carved beside it —
> the hands, the folded cloth, the bowed shoulders, beautifully made. Where each face should be
> there is a smooth blank oval, polished brighter than the rest.

**Truth.** The faces were struck off at the Queen's order so hers would be the only face here.

**The clue is automatic.** Anyone who looks sees the chisel marks are *inside* the barrow —
someone did this from below. `[VANITY: Observation — 1 success]` buys *extra*: the marks are
three centuries old, and made by many different hands.

**The decision — three ways in.**

| Route | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Servants' Stair** | narrow, slow — **1 Turn** | Arrive unnoticed |
| **Court Door** | grand, announced | Host **Aware** from A2; roll the clock immediately |
| **The Crack** | `[VANITY: Brawn — 2 successes]` | Failure banks a Bane, lands you noisily (as Court Door) |

**Failure case.** No choice = the Court Door. Say "you take the big doors, then" aloud.

---

### AREA 2 — The Antechamber of Bows
*Encounter: skirmish · the flattery route opens · Banes: +1 per flatterer*

> *(read aloud or paraphrase)* Nine courtiers stand bent at the waist in a bow, holding it. Their
> silks have rotted to cobweb; the gold thread has not. They are bowing to the middle of an empty
> room.

**Truth.** They bow to where the Queen *stood*. Three will move; six are just dead.

**Consequence.** Three **Skeletons** — atk 3, **def 2**, Grit 1, Nerve 5, fearless, half harm from
arrows. Heat: **skirmish**. They animate if struck, jostled, or robbed.

**The other route — bow back.** `[VANITY: Flair — 2 successes]` as a genuine court greeting.
**One hero suffices.** On success the Host resumes its bow and the party are **guests**: Skeletons
will not open hostilities first.

> **Flattery is repeatable.** Any hero may bow in *any* later area to be received. Each time:
> **+1 Vanity now, +1 Bane on the tab.** Shine now, bill later.
>
> **The signal.** From the first successful bow, the Host addresses flatterers by invented titles
> — *"my lord of the wet cloak"*. Players must be able to *see* the route is open and repeatable;
> in v1 they could not, and it killed them.

**Temptation — the Gilded Mask.**
- *Cue:* gold leaf over lime-wood, mouth fixed in a small smile.
- *Benefit:* automatic success on any bow-back, anywhere.
- *Accept:* **free.** *Use:* **+1 Bane** each time it saves you.
- *Standing:* while worn you cannot be recognised by name — allies' aid rolls that need to know
  you take −1 die.

**Failure case.** Fighting through without bowing is legitimate and harder — but A6 now always
offers a way in (see the Vice), so it is no longer a death sentence.

---

### AREA 3 — The Hall of Little Mirrors
*Faction contact · optional encounter · Banes: +1 per mirror-noticing*

> *(read aloud or paraphrase)* The walls are set with hundreds of hand-mirrors, no two the same,
> all angled inward. In the middle of them four living people sit back-to-back around a dead
> lantern, and every one of them is looking at you in a hundred pieces at once.

**Truth.** The Quill Gang. One man lost to the Wardrobe. They sat here to watch every approach,
not realising the mirrors are how the Host watches *them*.

**The clue is automatic.** The mirrors are angled *inward*, at the room, not outward at the doors.
`[VANITY: Observation — 1 success]` buys extra: some are angled at each other, endlessly.

**Consequence.** Roll **Reaction (2d6)** for Pell Quill.
- **Hostile:** 4 **Bandits** (atk 3, def 3, Grit 2, **Nerve 2**). **They fight one round, then
  break** — three days past brave. Do not run this as a full fight; it costs 15 minutes the
  schedule does not have.
- **Wary / Neutral:** they trade. They know the Wardrobe's door is the trap, and how.
- **Friendly:** they'll guide for a share.

**The mirror rule.** Anything reflected twice is **noticed** — roll the clock, and the hero who
lingered, posed or studied their reflection takes **+1 Bane**. Say the Bane out loud; this is the
tab's main engine outside flattery.

**The decision.** Ally, rob, or abandon. Drawing on the gang takes **+1 Bane** — the Host does not
care for guests who behave badly in the house.

**Failure case.** Gang dead or gone → A5's trap fires unwarned. Visibly the party's doing.

---

### AREA 4 — The Weeping Gallery
*Encounter: fight · no reward · Banes: —*

> *(read aloud or paraphrase)* A long gallery of portraits, floor to ceiling, all of the same
> woman. In every one the eyes have been scratched out — not slashed, *worked at*, patiently, by
> fingernails.

**Truth.** The court did this, two centuries in, unable to bear being looked at. Then they ate
each other.

**Consequence.** **One Ghoul** — atk 4, **def 2** (gorged, slow), Grit 4, Nerve 5. On a hit, a free
Called Shot to freeze the victim for a round. It is **slow**: a fighter can dictate range. Heat:
**fight**. It attacks anyone in court dress or wearing the Gilded Mask first.

**Temptation — none.** This area gives nothing. That is its job.

**Failure case.** Cuttable. If cut, move a scratched portrait into A5 so the motif completes.

---

### AREA 5 — The Wardrobe of the Court
*No encounter · the hoard · the choice that sets the finale · Banes: +1 per relic use*

> *(read aloud or paraphrase)* Every dress the court ever wore, on three hundred hangers, in a
> room with no dust in it at all. At the far end, facing away from the door, one covered frame the
> height of a person.

**Truth.** Uncovering the portrait wakes the Queen — it is the only place she can be seen.

**Consequence.** Hoard: **chest** tier. **The door is the trap**, not the frame: it closes on
whatever reaches past the threshold `[VANITY: Brawn or Flair — 2 successes; failure = Maimed]`.
Forewarned parties simply wedge it.

**In the hoard, always: *the Cantor's silvered blade*.** Plain, old, faintly warm. **It harms the
incorporeal.** Put it in front of the party's mundane fighter — without it, A6 has no room for them.

**Temptation — Dorian's ever-young portrait.**
- *Cue:* a young woman in gold, painted so well she seems about to speak.
- *Benefit:* you do not age; once per session **refuse a condition** — the portrait takes it,
  visibly. *Accept:* **free.** *Use:* **+1 Bane.**
- *Standing:* the Queen always knows where it is.

**The decision.**

| Choice | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Uncover and take it** | The Queen wakes **here**, on her ground. A6 comes to you. |
| **Leave it covered** | You reach A6 on your terms, with nothing she wants. |

**Failure case.** Taking it and running = the finale in the Mourners' Steps. The blank faces make
a better room for it anyway.

---

### AREA 6 — The Gilded Audience
### ⟨ AUTHORED SLOT — this is the seam ⟩

**Areas 1–5 are what DELVE generates. This is what it hands over.** A worked example; swap freely.

> *(read aloud or paraphrase)* The throne room is full of a court that has stopped pretending. On
> the throne, in a gown that still holds its shape, sits a woman-shaped absence — a gap in the air
> wearing gold. She turns towards you and there is nothing in the collar to turn.

**The Gilded Queen** — Wraith, **Grit 6**, atk 5 (chill touch, ignores armour, drains 1 max Grit),
def 3, Nerve 5, Resolve (Nemesis). **Incorporeal.**

> **Say this before initiative, always:** she is not solid; steel passes through her. **Blessed,
> silvered or magical weapons harm her** — Wren's mace, the Cantor's blade from A5, Yrsa's spells.
> In v1 the table discovered this by failing, and it was fatal. Never let that happen.

**She opens by asking:** *"How do I look?"*

| Party's route | The audience |
|---|---|
| **Anyone bowed, even once** | She listens. She trades the portrait for a *true* description — someone must look at her and say what is actually there. `[VANITY: Nerve — 2 successes]` or take **Rattled**. |
| **Nobody bowed, tab under 4** | She **offers a Vice** — *"Be beautiful for me and I will be generous"*: +1 Vanity, +1 Bane, and the conversation opens. Refusing is allowed, and starts the fight. |
| **Carrying the portrait** | She wants it more than you. Offering it ends everything — she looks at herself and comes apart. You leave with all but the prize. |
| **Fighting** | Grit 6, no reinforcements. Winnable by two engaged heroes in ~4 rounds. |

**The Reckoning.** If the tab reads six mid-audience, stop and roll it. A delve about vanity
billing you in front of the Queen of Vanity is the best ending this has; do not soften it.

**Failure case.** Fleeing with the portrait: they win and are haunted. She follows, and any later
session may open with the question again.
