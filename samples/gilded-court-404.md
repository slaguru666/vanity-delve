## The truth (GM only)

**The Cairn of the Patient Lord.** The Patient Lord bound a procession by oath they could not break, so that someone would always go behind them. The Patient Lord is second now, and cannot bear it. The prize is the crown worn first, and only once — the proof of precedence. The Silent Host are still performing the ritual they were buried for; they are handled by courtesy. The motif is **precedence**, and the danger is procession: the barrow keeps its order, and you have joined the queue.

**They will ask you:** "Who goes first?"

## Pressure

- **The clock.** Roll 1d6 on a trigger, or every 3 Turns. **1 or under — something comes.** Telegraph one Turn ahead.
- **Triggers this delve:** announced · loud · seenTwice · disturbed
- **The tab.** Target **5 Banes** by the ending. Banes are *offered by named beats*, never hoped for.
- **The danger is procession:** the barrow keeps its order, and you have joined the queue.

## The way through — the appeasement

Anyone may **stand aside and let it go ahead of you**. `[VANITY: Poise — 2 successes]`
On success it goes first, and The Silent Host will not open hostilities first.
**Cost: +1 Vanity now, +1 Bane on the tab.** Shine now, bill later.
**Repeatable in every area.** Say the Bane aloud when it banks — the table must be able to see the route is open.

## Trigger glossary

- **announced** — entered somewhere grandly — the big doors, a shout, a herald
- **seenTwice** — reflected, echoed or witnessed twice over in one place
- **loud** — a raised voice, a broken door, steel on stone
- **lingering** — stayed in one place longer than it takes to search it
- **disturbed** — struck or moved something that had not moved in centuries
