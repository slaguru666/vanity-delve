## The truth (GM only)

**The Vault of the Silent Margrave.** The Silent Margrave bought the young from those with nothing left, so that nothing would ever age. The Silent Margrave is old now, and cannot bear it. The prize is a sealed jar holding one good year — the only thing here that has not aged. The Silent Host are still performing the ritual they were buried for; they are handled by courtesy. The motif is **keeping**, and the danger is preservation: nothing here is allowed to change, including you.

**They will ask you:** "Tell me I have not changed."

## Pressure

- **The clock.** Roll 1d6 on a trigger, or every 3 Turns. **1 or under — something comes.** Telegraph one Turn ahead.
- **Triggers this delve:** disturbed · lingering · seenTwice
- **The tab.** Target **5 Banes** by the ending. Banes are *offered by named beats*, never hoped for.
- **The danger is preservation:** nothing here is allowed to change, including you.

## The way through — the appeasement

Anyone may **tell it that it has not changed**. `[VANITY: Flair — 2 successes]`
On success it hears that nothing has aged, and The Silent Host will not open hostilities first.
**Cost: +1 Vanity now, +1 Bane on the tab.** Shine now, bill later.
**Repeatable in every area.** Say the Bane aloud when it banks — the table must be able to see the route is open.

## Factions

- **The Silent Host** (undead) — still performing the ritual they were buried for. Handled by *courtesy*.
- **the Barrow-Wardens** (undead) — sealed this place and stayed to make sure. Handled by *proof of right*.

**Planned fights:** 3 of 6 areas · base heat *fight*

## Trigger glossary

- **announced** — entered somewhere grandly — the big doors, a shout, a herald
- **seenTwice** — reflected, echoed or witnessed twice over in one place
- **loud** — a raised voice, a broken door, steel on stone
- **lingering** — stayed in one place longer than it takes to search it
- **disturbed** — struck or moved something that had not moved in centuries
