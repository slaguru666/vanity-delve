## The truth (GM only)

**The Cairn of the Peacock Prince.** The Peacock Prince bound servants by oath they could not break, so that someone would always be there. The Peacock Prince is alone now, and cannot bear it. The prize is a keepsake they would not be parted from — the promise that someone would stay. The Silent Host are still performing the ritual they were buried for; they are handled by courtesy. The motif is **company**, and the danger is attachment: the barrow does not like it when people leave.

**They will ask you:** "You are not leaving."

## Pressure

- **The clock.** Roll 1d6 on a trigger, or every 3 Turns. **1 or under — something comes.** Telegraph one Turn ahead.
- **Triggers this delve:** lingering · seenTwice · loud · disturbed
- **The tab.** Target **5 Banes** by the ending. Banes are *offered by named beats*, never hoped for.
- **The danger is attachment:** the barrow does not like it when people leave.

## The way through — the appeasement

Anyone may **sit down and stay a while**. `[VANITY: Poise — 2 successes]`
On success it is not alone, and The Silent Host will not open hostilities first.
**Cost: +1 Vanity now, +1 Bane on the tab.** Shine now, bill later.
**Repeatable in every area.** Say the Bane aloud when it banks — the table must be able to see the route is open.

## Factions

- **The Silent Host** (undead) — still performing the ritual they were buried for. Handled by *courtesy*.
- **grave-robbers** (living) — got in first and cannot get out. Handled by *trade*.

**Planned fights:** 3 of 6 areas · base heat *fight*

## Trigger glossary

- **announced** — entered somewhere grandly — the big doors, a shout, a herald
- **seenTwice** — reflected, echoed or witnessed twice over in one place
- **loud** — a raised voice, a broken door, steel on stone
- **lingering** — stayed in one place longer than it takes to search it
- **disturbed** — struck or moved something that had not moved in centuries
