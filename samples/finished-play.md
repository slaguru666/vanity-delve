# The Vault of the Patient Lord

> They bought a household from people with nothing left, so that someone would always obey. The household is still obeying.

**Appease:** do one small thing it asks, without arguing `[Poise 2]` — +1 Vanity now, +1 Bane later. Repeatable, every area.
**Clock:** 1d6 on a trigger. **1 — something comes.** **Tab:** 5 by the end.
**GM truth:** The Patient Lord bought a household from those with nothing left, so that someone would always obey. The Patient Lord is disobeyed now, and cannot bear it.

---

### 1 · The Weeping Stair

> Forty steps down and the bones are still on their errand — a tray here, a folded cloth there, set down mid-stride three hundred years ago. Somebody was talking as you came through the arch, and stopped.

**Here:** Three of the Host in servants' dress, carrying out an errand that has no destination left — they stop, and wait to be told what to do. **They can:** tell them where to go, and they go.
**An order carved into the floor, still legible** — `[Poise 2]` you obey exactly enough to be counted household — Skeletons stand down for you · **miss** you obey wrongly, which is worse than not obeying: +1 Bane · **or** step over it: no cost now, and the Host does not count you as theirs

### 2 · The Lesser Vault

> The floor is scratched over with orders, the last one unfinished. A door at the far end is braced shut from the inside with furniture, and behind you a living man is whispering the same phrase again and again, trying to get the sound of it right.

**Here:** A living grave-robber, repeating a phrase over and over, trying to get the intonation right — they go quiet and put their back to the wall. **They can:** trade something for the phrase, badly remembered.
**Two doors, and behind one a voice giving instructions to nobody** — `[Poise 2]` you answer the voice as a subordinate would — it tells you the route AND answers one question about what lies on it, without taking you into its service · **miss** you engage and it takes that as enlistment: it will give you orders for the rest of the delve, and the first is now · **or** take the quiet door and never speak to it: one extra Turn, no obligation
**Fight:** 1× Ghoul `4/3/4` · 2× Skeleton `3/2/1` — harmed by anything.
**Tube:** ask one question and get one true answer. *Use: +1 Bane. whatever answers can now hear you.*

### 3 · The Crossing

> The hall answers before you do — your own voice reaches the far wall a half-beat early, saying what you have not said yet. Somewhere below a bell starts, and then the one in this room takes it up.

**Here:** The bells, ringing a summons room by room, coming closer — the bell in this room begins. **They can:** answer the bell yourself before whatever is meant to arrives.
**A bell-pull that would summon someone** — `[Poise 2]` what comes is answerable to you for one scene · **miss** what comes is not the kind of help you wanted · **or** do not ring: the way on is longer by one Turn

### 4 · The Servants' Deep

> An instruction has been cut into the stone so many times the stone gave out. Bootprints cross the floor and simply stop. At the far wall a figure is talking steadily into a speaking-tube, reporting to somebody who died first.

**Here:** A single figure at a speaking-tube, reporting, at length, to a listener who died first — it pauses mid-sentence and asks whether you are the reply. **They can:** answer as the listener would, and learn what it has been reporting.
**A door that opens to a spoken password nobody living knows** — `[Wits 2]` the word is already in your notes. It is in something you passed earlier — *someone has been talking here, and stopped when you arrived*. Say it and the door opens; if nobody wrote it down, the roll tells them now · **miss** you have the shape of it but not the word: one more attempt, or take the long way · **or** the long way: two Turns, no guessing, no Banes
**Bell:** ring it and one order is obeyed, by anything. *Use: +2 Banes. it rings faintly when someone gives you an order.*

### 5 · The Last Door

> Wax has been jammed into the tube here, still soft. A key that fits nothing hangs on a nail. One of them is kneeling at the last door, and has been for a very long time, waiting to be told what to do.

**Here:** One of the Host, kneeling, waiting for an instruction that was promised and never came — it addresses the first of you to speak as its superior. **They can:** give it the instruction and it will carry it out to the letter, wherever that leads.
**An instruction you can obey, ignore, or countermand** — `[Poise 2]` you countermand it and the dead obey you instead — this marks you as someone who gives orders: from now on the Host brings you their disputes, and once per delve you may issue one order they must follow · **miss** the order stands and you have been seen to fail at giving one: +1 Bane, and the Host addresses someone else from now on · **or** obey it, or ignore it and go round: ignoring costs one Turn
**Battle:** 1× Wraith `5/3/10` · 3× Skeleton `3/2/1` — harmed by blessed, silvered or magical weapons ONLY.
**Staff:** give one order the dead must follow. *Use: +2 Banes. you may not refuse an order given to you.*

### The Audience

> The seat at the head of the hall is occupied by a shape that keeps the gown's outline and nothing else. It turns toward you and there is nothing in the collar to turn.

**It asks:** "Do as you are told."
**The Patient Lord** — Wraith, Grit 6, atk 5, def 3, incorporeal. Blessed, silvered or magical weapons only; say so before initiative. It opens with "Do as you are told." Obey one small instruction and it will trade the signet. Refuse and it is a fight it will win slowly.
