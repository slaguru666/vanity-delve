## The truth (GM only)

**The Vault of the Patient Lord.** The Patient Lord bought a household from those with nothing left, so that someone would always obey. The Patient Lord is disobeyed now, and cannot bear it. The prize is the signet that is still obeyed — the only thing the household answers to. The Silent Host are still performing the ritual they were buried for; they are handled by courtesy. The motif is **voices**, and the danger is obedience: the dead here still take instruction, and give it.

**They will ask you:** "Do as you are told."

## Pressure

- **The clock.** Roll 1d6 on a trigger, or every 3 Turns. **1 or under — something comes.** Telegraph one Turn ahead.
- **Triggers this delve:** seenTwice · loud · disturbed
- **The tab.** Target **5 Banes** by the ending. Banes are *offered by named beats*, never hoped for.
- **The danger is obedience:** the dead here still take instruction, and give it.

## The way through — the appeasement

Anyone may **do one small thing it asks, without arguing**. `[VANITY: Poise — 2 successes]`
On success it was obeyed, and The Silent Host will not open hostilities first.
**Cost: +1 Vanity now, +1 Bane on the tab.** Shine now, bill later.
**Repeatable in every area.** Say the Bane aloud when it banks — the table must be able to see the route is open.
