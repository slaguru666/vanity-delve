> *the dead here still take instruction, and give it.*

## The truth (GM only)

**The Vault of the Patient Lord.** The Patient Lord bought a household from those with nothing left, so that someone would always obey. The Patient Lord is disobeyed now, and cannot bear it. The prize is the signet that is still obeyed — the only thing the household answers to. The Silent Host are still performing the ritual they were buried for; they are handled by courtesy. The motif is **voices**, and the danger is obedience: the dead here still take instruction, and give it.

**They will ask you:** "Do as you are told."

## Pressure

- **The clock.** Roll 1d6 on a trigger, or every 3 Turns. **1 or under — something comes.** Telegraph one Turn ahead.
- **Triggers this delve:** seenTwice · loud · disturbed
- **The tab.** Target **5 Banes** by the ending. Banes are *offered by named beats*, never hoped for.
- **The danger is obedience:** the dead here still take instruction, and give it.

## The way through — the appeasement

Anyone may **do one small thing it asks, without arguing**. `[VANITY: Poise — 2 successes]`
On success it was obeyed, and The Silent Host will not open hostilities first.
**Cost: +1 Vanity now, +1 Bane on the tab.** Shine now, bill later.
**Repeatable in every area.** Say the Bane aloud when it banks — the table must be able to see the route is open.

## Factions

- **The Silent Host** (undead) — still performing the ritual they were buried for. Handled by *courtesy*.
- **things that moved in** (beast) — neither know nor care whose tomb this is. Handled by *force or food*.

**Planned fights:** 2 of 5 areas · base heat *fight*

### AREA 1 — The Weeping Stair
*approach · facet: institution · no encounter*

**Cue** — *assemble aloud; these are fragments, not read-aloud text*
- servants' bones arranged as though mid-errand
- someone has been talking here, and stopped when you arrived

**The situation.** three of the Host in servants' dress — carrying out an errand that has no destination left.
**When you walk in:** they stop, and wait to be told what to do.
- *Why (GM):* the order was never rescinded and nobody outranks it
- *What it offers:* tell them where to go, and they go

**Truth.** this is how the place was ordered, and it still is. The Patient Lord bought a household from those with nothing left, so that someone would always obey.

**The clue is automatic:** servants' bones arranged as though mid-errand — and it was done deliberately.
[VANITY: Observation — 1 success] buys who did it, and roughly when

**The decision.** an order carved into the floor, still legible — *obey it and the Host counts you as household; step over it and they do not*
- **Attempt it:** `[VANITY: Poise 2]`
- **Success:** you obey exactly enough to be counted household — Skeletons stand down for you
- **Failure:** you obey wrongly, which is worse than not obeying: +1 Bane
- **Or simply:** step over it: no cost now, and the Host does not count you as theirs


**Attention trigger:** seenTwice.
**Bane beat — announcing.** Who: *the whole party*. When: *once, on entering loudly*. once only. Say it aloud when it banks.
**Fallback route.** The Silent Host will receive anyone who behaves as a guest — cost: one Bane on the tab, and they will expect it again.

*Failure case.* If they skip this, move its cue fragment into the next area so the motif still completes.

## Trigger glossary

- **announced** — entered somewhere grandly — the big doors, a shout, a herald
- **seenTwice** — reflected, echoed or witnessed twice over in one place
- **loud** — a raised voice, a broken door, steel on stone
- **lingering** — stayed in one place longer than it takes to search it
- **disturbed** — struck or moved something that had not moved in centuries
