**IMAGE** orders scratched into the floor, the last one unfinished · a door braced shut from within by furniture
**SITUATION** A living grave-robber — repeating a phrase over and over, trying to get the intonation right. On arrival, they go quiet and put their back to the wall.
**WHY** They heard the password and cannot say it correctly. **OFFERS** trade something for the phrase, badly remembered.
**DECISION** Two doors, and behind one a voice giving instructions to nobody — *the quiet door is longer; the voice would tell you exactly where to go*

  · **miss** → you engage and it takes that as enlistment: it will give you orders for the rest of the delve, and the first is now
  · **or** take the quiet door and never speak to it: one extra Turn, no obligation

  · take free · use +1 Bane · standing: whatever answers can now hear you

## Area 5 — The Last Door
`threshold · anchor · battle · chest`
*reroll:* `--reroll=5:situation` `--reroll=5:decision` `--reroll=5:temptation` `--reroll=5:feature` `--reroll=5:name`

**IMAGE** a key that fits nothing here · wax jammed into a speaking-tube, still soft
**SITUATION** One of the Host, kneeling — waiting for an instruction that was promised and never came. On arrival, it addresses the first of you to speak as its superior.
**WHY** It was told to await further orders and did. **OFFERS** give it the instruction and it will carry it out to the letter, wherever that leads.
**DECISION** An instruction you can obey, ignore, or countermand — *countermanding it works once and marks you as someone who gives orders*

  · **miss** → the order stands and you have been seen to fail at giving one: +1 Bane, and the Host addresses someone else from now on
  · **or** obey it, or ignore it and go round: ignoring costs one Turn
**ENCOUNTER** 1 Wraith and 3 Skeletons — 1× Wraith atk5/def3/G10, 3× Skeleton atk3/def2/G1

**TEMPTATION** Staff — a staff of office, propped where it was set down: give one order the dead must follow
  · take the Vice Pride · use +2 Banes · standing: you may not refuse an order given to you
**PRESSURE** trigger disturbed · bane beat refusal · fallback The Silent Host will receive anyone who behaves as a guest
